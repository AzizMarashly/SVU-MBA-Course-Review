Wil describes his business as follows: 

A. I count the number of Douglas Fir Christmas trees in the field (24,000). 

B. Next, I agree on a contract lump sum for shearing with a crew boss for the whole field 
($30,000). 

C. When partial payment for work completed arrives (5 days later), I count or estimate the 
actual number sheared (6,000 trees). I take the actual as a percent of the total to be 
sheared and multiply the percent complete by total contract amount for the partial payment 
[(6,000/$30,000 = 25%), (.25 × $30,000 = $7500)].
